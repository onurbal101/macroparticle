# Feature inventory

All rows use TR09 unless a different source is stated. The canonical dataset contains every program and generates the app and the print index.

| Control | Physical position | Function / manual page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Healthy Steam | row 1 left | Two-level 17-program menu, pp. 15-16 |
| Auto Cook | row 1 right | 20 fixed programs, pp. 18-20 |

Healthy group 1 has codes 1-1 through 1-11. Asian group 2 has codes 2-1 through 2-6. Auto Cook has codes 1-20. Quick Defrost has codes 1-5. Equal numbers in different functions are distinct records.

Other covered operations include quick start at 800 W, adding time, clockwise/counterclockwise duration adjustment, 12/24 h clock, completion signals, automatic energy saving, saving and recalling appliance memories, cookware, steamer, cleaning, storage, setup, water test, troubleshooting and C-d0.

Sound on/off is listed by PRODUCT. The /ND procedure remains unconfirmed. Regional English evidence is reported in the app. No operational combination is asserted as confirmed.

PRODUCT and TR09 p. 28 confirm a 23 L solo unit, 800 W output, 1250 W input, six powers, glass steamer and 288 mm turntable (PRODUCT). PRODUCT explicitly says no sensor cooking, steam cleaning, turntable switch or kitchen timer.
